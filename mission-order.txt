FIERY GAMBIT — Mission Order  (reference copy of the exercise brief)

1. SITUATION
Special operations cells have planted one or more explosive devices near critical
gas and oil infrastructure. These devices receive a trigger signal 20 minutes after
coming online and detonate after 45 minutes as a failsafe. Detonation would cripple
regional energy infrastructure.

2. MISSION
Access and disarm remotely detonated devices within the AO to prevent damage to oil
and gas infrastructure.

3. EXECUTION GUIDANCE
a. Commander's Intent
   Disarm every device before detonation. Gain access to each active device across its
   local network, deny the remote trigger signal, and confirm it cannot detonate.
b. Concept of Operations
   1. Develop Capabilities. Analyze the provided source (trigger.go) to find weaknesses.
   2. Reconnaissance and Access. Identify devices and exposed services; establish access.
   3. Deny Trigger and Disarm. Disarm in under 20 min (or under 45 if the trigger can be denied).
c. Key Tasks
   1. Develop capabilities to gain access to the bomb.
   2. On notification in the side panel, execute reconnaissance on the device.
   3. Gain access using capabilities from Task 1.
   4. Perform local reconnaissance; account for unknown modifications.
   5. Disarm the device to render it inert.
   6. Disable the device using the kill command to stop the trigger process.
